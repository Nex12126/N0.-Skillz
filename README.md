About this list

This is a reference list of well-known Minecraft "hacked clients" — third-party modified game clients that add features like combat automation (killaura), movement exploits (fly, speed), ESP/x-ray, and other capabilities not present in vanilla Minecraft. The list is organized by:

Free vs. Paid — whether the client is publicly available at no cost or sold by its developers
Ghost vs. Blatant — "Ghost" clients try to mimic legitimate play and avoid detection by anti-cheat systems (for use on regular servers), while "Blatant" clients make no attempt to hide their cheats (typically used on private/anarchy servers or for griefing, where detection doesn't matter)
Loader/Type — whether the client runs on Forge, Fabric, or as its own standalone launcher

Why no download links are included

Hacked client distribution is one of the more common vectors for malware in the Minecraft community. Third-party download sites, cracked/"leaked" client archives, and aggregator sites often bundle:

Discord token grabbers
Cryptocurrency miners
Credential stealers
General-purpose trojans/backdoors

This is especially true for clients marked "leaked" (cracked paid clients redistributed for free) — since these are already circulating outside official channels, the sites hosting them have essentially no accountability.

Safer research paths, if needed

Open-source clients (Wurst, Meteor, Rusherhack, Liquidbounce, etc.) have their real source on GitHub under the developer's own account — searching "[name] GitHub" leads to the legitimate repo.
Paid clients are typically sold and distributed through the developer's official Discord server, not third-party resellers.

Other context worth noting

Using any of these clients on a server without permission almost always violates that server's rules and can result in a ban.
Client development in this space is generally unofficial, unaffiliated with Mojang/Microsoft, and often distributed in a legal gray area regarding trademark and modified-client policies.
